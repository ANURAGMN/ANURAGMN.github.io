Privacy Policy — EduAI

Last updated: July 29, 2026

EduAI (“NCERT Class 7 AI Tutor & Labs”, package com.ncert7.aitutorandlab) is operated by PADAAMS. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account: name, email (Google Sign-In or institutional @padaams.in sign-in), class level, language preference.
  • Learning progress: concepts completed, simulations, streaks, exam-plan and trial progress — stored on your device and synced to Firebase when you are signed in.
  • Usage analytics: screen views, learning clicks, funnel events — used to improve the product.
  • Microphone: only when you use voice input in the tutor; audio is processed for speech recognition and is not stored as recordings by default.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may collect device/ad identifiers to show child-safe banner and rewarded ads.
  • Crash & error logs: Firebase Crashlytics and Firestore error logs to fix bugs.

How we use information

  • Provide tutoring, simulations, progress tracking, and gamification (XP, gems, leagues, friends).
  • Sync progress across sessions on the same account.
  • Show age-appropriate ads after free daily interactions.
  • Improve reliability, security, and learning features.

Sharing

We use Google (Sign-In, Firebase, AdMob, Play services), our AI backend, and cloud hosting to run the app. We do not sell personal information. Friend features show display names and activity milestones only to linked friends.

Children

EduAI is designed for school learners. Parents or guardians should supervise account creation and use. Ads are configured for child-directed, G-rated content per Google Play and AdMob policies.

Retention & deletion

Progress and profile data remain while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us; uninstalling the app removes local data from your device.
